Distance & Honesty

Our Story

Chapter 1

Between the Miles

For a long time, everything we had lived in phone screens, late-night audio notes, and the constant ache of geography. We spent months sharing our thoughts across miles, building an entire world out of words.

We never got to experience the simple things that most people take for granted — we never had a proper first date, never took a spontaneous weekend trip together, and never got to walk side by side down a quiet street while we were together.

"We didn't get the timing right, but that doesn't mean what was felt wasn't real."

Chapter 2

After the Breakup

When we finally met in person, it was after the breakup. Life had already moved our paths in different directions. And yet, seeing you face to face wasn't about reopening wounds — it was a quiet reminder of how genuine you are, how deeply you care, and why you will always matter to me.

That meeting wasn't about the past. It was about seeing the person you became, and recognizing something that never changed — your kindness, your strength, the light you carry even when you think no one is watching.

Chapter 3

What I Carry With Me

Today isn't about looking backward with regret, and it isn't an attempt to rewrite where things stand. It is simply me taking a moment to honor the person you are, and to tell you that having known you is something I will always carry with gratitude.

You taught me things about patience, about vulnerability, and about how deeply one person can care for another across thousands of miles. I won't forget that.

— Abyss ♡

On Your Birthday

Happy Birthday.

I wanted to give you something genuine this year — something that isn’t wrapped up in expectations, demands, or awkwardness. Just a space dedicated to you, because today is your day, and you deserve to feel celebrated.

I won’t pretend that everything between us was simple. Long distance wasn't kind to us. We spent so much time holding onto conversations through a screen, wishing for normal moments that never arrived in the way we hoped. And when we finally got to see each other in person, it was after things had already come to an end.

I still love you. That truth doesn't just disappear, and I won't dishonor what we shared by pretending otherwise. But I am not writing this to ask you for anything. I'm not asking you to come back, I’m not asking for a conversation, and you do not owe me any reply to this page. Love, at its best, isn’t a transaction. It’s simply wishing someone everything good, even from afar.

I just wanted you to know that having known you made a difference in my life. You taught me things about patience, about vulnerability, and about how deeply one person can care for another across thousands of miles.

I hope this next year of your life treats you gently. I hope you achieve every small goal you keep private in your notes, that you find peace in the choices you make, and that you never forget how worthy of love and happiness you are.

Thank you for being part of my story, Asmita. I wish you the happiest of birthdays.

Always with love and respect, Abyss ♡
